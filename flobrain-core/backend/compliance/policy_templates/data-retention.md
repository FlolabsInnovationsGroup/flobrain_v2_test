## 1. Principle

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, for a contract, or because
the law requires it. Then we delete it.

## 2. Periods

| Information | Kept for | How it is deleted |
|---|---|---|
| Room messages | {{MESSAGES_DAYS}} days | Retention job in FloBrain, daily |
| Meeting recordings | {{RECORDINGS_DAYS}} days | Retention job in FloBrain, daily |
| Meeting transcripts | {{TRANSCRIPTS_DAYS}} days | Retention job in FloBrain, daily |
| Time entries | {{TIME_DAYS}} days | Retention job in FloBrain, daily |
| Audit log | at least 12 months | Kept by FloBrain; exported with evidence packages |
| Evidence packages | 7 days after they are made | Deleted automatically |
| Former members' data | until a deletion request is approved, or the periods above | Deletion request in FloBrain |

## 3. Requests from people

People can download their data and ask for deletion from the Privacy page. Admins decide deletion requests
within 30 days. Approved deletions are recorded in the audit log.

## 4. Legal holds

When there is a dispute or an investigation, the policy owner can pause deletion for the information involved.
The hold is written down and lifted when it is no longer needed.

## 5. Backups

Backups follow the hosting provider's schedule and expire on their own. Deleted data disappears from backups
when they expire.
