# Acceptable use policy (including AI use)

**Organization:** {{ORG_NAME}}
**Owner:** {{POLICY_OWNER}}
**Effective:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}
**Review:** every 12 months

> Template. Adjust it to your setup before you publish it.

## 1. Company systems and information

- Use company systems for work. Light personal use is fine if it does not affect work or security.
- Keep your devices locked when you step away, up to date, and protected by a password or biometric.
- Do not install software from untrusted sources on devices that hold company data.
- Do not copy company or customer data to personal accounts or storage.

## 2. Customer and personal data

- Only open customer or personal data when your task needs it.
- Share it only with people and tools that are allowed to see it.
- Report a mistake (wrong recipient, lost device) at once. Nobody is punished for reporting.

## 3. Using AI

- **Approved tools only.** Use the AI buddies in FloBrain and other tools on the approved list. Do not paste
  customer data, secrets or personal data into AI tools that are not approved.
- **You stay responsible.** Check what the AI writes before you use it. AI can be wrong or invent facts.
- **Approvals are real decisions.** When a buddy proposes an action (send an email, create an issue, update a
  record), read the preview before you approve it. If you are not sure, reject it.
- **No secrets in prompts.** Never put passwords, keys or tokens in a prompt or a buddy's instructions.
- **Say when AI wrote it** if the reader would expect to know, for example in customer replies.
- **Private-only data.** If the organization requires self-hosted AI only, do not work around it.

## 4. Communication

- Be respectful in rooms, chats and meetings. Harassment is not tolerated.
- Tell people before you record a meeting. FloBrain shows a banner while recording.

## 5. Breaking this policy

Misuse can lead to access being removed and to disciplinary action.
