## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how {{ORG_NAME}} protects the information it holds for its customers, its people
and itself. It is the top policy: the access control, acceptable use, incident response, data
retention, vendor management and business continuity policies sit under it.

## 2. Scope

Everyone who works for or with {{ORG_NAME}} (employees, contractors, interns) and every system that
stores or processes our information, including FloBrain and the AI buddies inside it.

## 3. Principles

- **Least privilege.** People get the access their work needs and no more. Access is reviewed every quarter.
- **People approve what AI does.** AI may draft, summarize and suggest. Anything that writes outside
  our workspace, sends a message to a customer or changes data on someone's behalf waits for a person's approval.
- **Encrypt by default.** Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and secrets are encrypted at rest.
- **Keep only what we need.** Retention periods are set and applied.
- **Record and review.** Admin and access changes are logged; logs are kept and reviewed.
- **Report early.** Anyone who sees something wrong reports it at once. Reporting is never punished.

## 4. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Policy owner ({{POLICY_OWNER}}) | Keeps this policy current, runs the yearly review, reports to leadership |
| Admins | Run access reviews, approve access requests, keep the trust page and evidence current |
| Team leads | Review their team's access each quarter |
| Everyone | Follows the policies, acknowledges each new version, reports incidents |

## 5. Risk management

We keep a short risk list: what could go wrong, how likely, how bad, what we do about it, and who owns
it. We review it at least once a year and after every serious incident.

## 6. Compliance and exceptions

Exceptions are written down with a reason, an owner and an end date, and approved by the policy owner.
Breaking a policy on purpose can lead to disciplinary action.

## 7. Acknowledgement

Every member acknowledges this policy when they join and each time a new version is published.
